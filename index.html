About Me

I am the creator of Digital Canvas, a web-based drawing application that allows users to express their creativity through digital art. I'm passionate about making creative tools accessible to everyone.

The Digital Canvas project features:

  • Intuitive drawing tools
  • Customizable brush sizes
  • Color palette selection
  • Easy saving of artwork
